Operations & Expressions

Side A — Numerical Expressions

Simplify the following numerical expressions.

1.

Simplify: \[ 5\!\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right) - 3\!\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) + 7\!\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) + 1 \]

Solution.

Multiply each term: \[ -\frac{5}{3} + \frac{3}{2} - \frac{14}{3} + 1 \]

Convert to a common denominator of 6: \[ -\frac{10}{6} + \frac{9}{6} - \frac{28}{6} + \frac{6}{6} \]

Combine numerators: \[ \frac{-10 + 9 - 28 + 6}{6} = \frac{-23}{6} = -\frac{23}{6} \]

2.

Simplify: \[ (-6)\cdot 3 \div (-2) - 8 \div (-4) \]

Solution.

Work left to right, applying multiplication and division in order: \[\begin{aligned} (-6)\cdot 3 &= -18 \\ -18 \div (-2) &= 9 \\ 8 \div (-4) &= -2 \end{aligned}\]

Subtract the second result: \[ 9 - (-2) = 9 + 2 = 11 \]

3.

Simplify: \[ 3(-2)^3 + 4(-2)^2 - 9(-2) - 14 \]

Solution.

Evaluate each power: \[ (-2)^3 = -8, \qquad (-2)^2 = 4 \]

Substitute and multiply: \[ 3(-8) + 4(4) - 9(-2) - 14 = -24 + 16 + 18 - 14 \]

Combine: \[ -4 \]

Side B — Algebraic Expressions

Simplify the expressions.

1.

Simplify: \(2(m-3)+5(m+1)\)

Solution.

Distribute: \[ 2m - 6 + 5m + 5 \]

Combine like terms: \[ 7m - 1 \]

2.

Simplify: \(-3(m-2n)+(m+2n)\)

Solution.

Distribute: \[ -3m + 6n + m + 2n \]

Combine like terms: \[ -2m + 8n \]

3.

Simplify: \(8xy^2 - 5x^2y + 2xy^2 + 7x^2y\)

Solution.

Group like terms (\(xy^2\) terms and \(x^2y\) terms): \[ (8xy^2 + 2xy^2) + (-5x^2y + 7x^2y) \]

Combine each group: \[ 10xy^2 + 2x^2y \]

4.

Evaluate \(5(x-2y)-3(2x+y)-2(x-y)\) when \(x = \dfrac{1}{3}\) and \(y = -\dfrac{3}{4}\).

Solution.

Distribute and collect like terms first: \[\begin{aligned} &= 5x - 10y - 6x - 3y - 2x + 2y \\ &= (5x - 6x - 2x) + (-10y - 3y + 2y) \\ &= -3x - 11y \end{aligned}\]

Substitute \(x = \dfrac{1}{3}\) and \(y = -\dfrac{3}{4}\): \[\begin{aligned} &= -3\!\left(\frac{1}{3}\right) - 11\!\left(-\frac{3}{4}\right) \\ &= -1 + \frac{33}{4} \\ &= -\frac{4}{4} + \frac{33}{4} = \frac{29}{4} \end{aligned}\]

5.

Evaluate \(3(x^2-1)-4(x^2+1)-(2x^2-1)\) when \(x = \dfrac{2}{3}\).

Solution.

Distribute and collect like terms first: \[\begin{aligned} &= 3x^2 - 3 - 4x^2 - 4 - 2x^2 + 1 \\ &= (3x^2 - 4x^2 - 2x^2) + (-3 - 4 + 1) \\ &= -3x^2 - 6 \end{aligned}\]

Substitute \(x = \dfrac{2}{3}\), so \(x^2 = \dfrac{4}{9}\): \[\begin{aligned} &= -3\!\left(\frac{4}{9}\right) - 6 \\ &= -\frac{12}{9} - 6 \\ &= -\frac{4}{3} - \frac{18}{3} = -\frac{22}{3} \end{aligned}\]
