    Section 1 Arithmetic of Rational Expressions

    Worksheet 1.1 Rational Expressions Worksheet
    

    Practice simplifying, multiplying, dividing, adding, and subtracting rational expressions. Assume all variables take values for which the expressions are defined.
    🔗

    1.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{-42x^3y^2}{-54x^2y^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Divide the coefficients by their greatest common factor, \(6\text{,}\) and cancel the common powers of the variables:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{-42x^3y^2}{-54x^2y^2} \amp= \dfrac{42}{54}\cdot\dfrac{x^3}{x^2}\cdot\dfrac{y^2}{y^2}\\ \amp= \dfrac{7}{9}\cdot x \cdot 1\\ \amp= \dfrac{7x}{9} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    2.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{x^2-7x}{x^2-49}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the numerator by taking out the GCF, and factor the denominator as a difference of squares:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{x^2-7x}{x^2-49} \amp= \dfrac{x(x-7)}{(x-7)(x+7)} \end{align*}
    Cancel the common factor \(x-7\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{x}{x+7} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    3.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{7n^2+23n+6}{21n^2-n-2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the numerator with the \(ac\) method: \(ac = 42\text{,}\) and the numbers \(21\) and \(2\) multiply to \(42\) and add to \(23\text{.}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 7n^2+23n+6 \amp= 7n^2+21n+2n+6\\ \amp= 7n(n+3)+2(n+3)\\ \amp= (n+3)(7n+2) \end{align*}
    Factor the denominator: \(ac = -42\text{,}\) and the numbers \(-7\) and \(6\) multiply to \(-42\) and add to \(-1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 21n^2-n-2 \amp= 21n^2-7n+6n-2\\ \amp= 7n(3n-1)+2(3n-1)\\ \amp= (3n-1)(7n+2) \end{align*}
    Cancel the common factor \(7n+2\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{(n+3)(7n+2)}{(3n-1)(7n+2)} = \dfrac{n+3}{3n-1} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    4.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{6a^2-17a+5}{15a-6a^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the numerator: \(ac = 30\text{,}\) and the numbers \(-15\) and \(-2\) multiply to \(30\) and add to \(-17\text{.}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 6a^2-17a+5 \amp= 6a^2-15a-2a+5\\ \amp= 3a(2a-5)-1(2a-5)\\ \amp= (2a-5)(3a-1) \end{align*}
    Factor the denominator. Taking out \(-3a\) makes the binomial match the one in the numerator:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 15a-6a^2 \amp= -3a(2a-5) \end{align*}
    Cancel the common factor \(2a-5\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{(2a-5)(3a-1)}{-3a(2a-5)} = \dfrac{3a-1}{-3a} = -\dfrac{3a-1}{3a} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    5.

    Multiply the rational expressions. \(\dfrac{x^2+x}{x+4}\cdot\dfrac{x^2-4x+3}{x^4-x^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor every numerator and denominator:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} x^2+x \amp= x(x+1)\\ x^2-4x+3 \amp= (x-1)(x-3)\\ x^4-x^2 \amp= x^2(x^2-1) = x^2(x-1)(x+1) \end{align*}
    Rewrite the product with the factored forms:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{x(x+1)}{x+4}\cdot\dfrac{(x-1)(x-3)}{x^2(x-1)(x+1)} \end{equation*}
    Cancel the common factors \(x\text{,}\) \(x+1\text{,}\) and \(x-1\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{x-3}{x(x+4)} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    6.

    Multiply the rational expressions. \(\dfrac{8x^2+10x-3}{6x^2+7x-3}\cdot\dfrac{3x^2+20x-7}{8x^2+18x-5}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor each quadratic using the \(ac\) method.
    🔗
    For \(8x^2+10x-3\text{,}\) \(ac=-24\) and the numbers are \(12\) and \(-2\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 8x^2+10x-3 \amp= 8x^2+12x-2x-3 = 4x(2x+3)-(2x+3) = (2x+3)(4x-1) \end{align*}
    For \(6x^2+7x-3\text{,}\) \(ac=-18\) and the numbers are \(9\) and \(-2\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 6x^2+7x-3 \amp= 6x^2+9x-2x-3 = 3x(2x+3)-(2x+3) = (2x+3)(3x-1) \end{align*}
    For \(3x^2+20x-7\text{,}\) \(ac=-21\) and the numbers are \(21\) and \(-1\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 3x^2+20x-7 \amp= 3x^2+21x-x-7 = 3x(x+7)-(x+7) = (x+7)(3x-1) \end{align*}
    For \(8x^2+18x-5\text{,}\) \(ac=-40\) and the numbers are \(20\) and \(-2\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 8x^2+18x-5 \amp= 8x^2+20x-2x-5 = 4x(2x+5)-(2x+5) = (2x+5)(4x-1) \end{align*}
    Substitute the factored forms and cancel \(2x+3\text{,}\) \(4x-1\text{,}\) and \(3x-1\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \amp \dfrac{(2x+3)(4x-1)}{(2x+3)(3x-1)}\cdot\dfrac{(x+7)(3x-1)}{(2x+5)(4x-1)}\\ \amp= \dfrac{x+7}{2x+5} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    7.

    Multiply the rational expressions. \(\dfrac{-14xy^4}{18y^2}\cdot\dfrac{24x^2y^3}{35y^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Multiply the numerators and the denominators:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{(-14xy^4)(24x^2y^3)}{(18y^2)(35y^2)} = \dfrac{-336x^3y^7}{630y^4} \end{equation*}
    The GCF of \(336\) and \(630\) is \(42\text{,}\) so the coefficient reduces to \(-\frac{8}{15}\text{.}\) Subtract the exponents of \(y\text{:}\) \(y^{7}/y^{4} = y^3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} -\dfrac{8x^3y^3}{15} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    8.

    Divide the rational expressions. \(\dfrac{7x^2y}{9xy^3}\div\dfrac{3x^4}{2x^2y^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Change division to multiplication by the reciprocal:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{7x^2y}{9xy^3}\cdot\dfrac{2x^2y^2}{3x^4} \end{equation*}
    Multiply across and combine the powers of each variable:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \amp= \dfrac{14x^4y^3}{27x^5y^3}\\ \amp= \dfrac{14}{27x} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    9.

    Divide the rational expressions. \(\dfrac{3x^2-20x+25}{2x^2-7x-15}\div\dfrac{9x^2-3x-20}{12x^2+28x+15}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor each quadratic.
    🔗
    For \(3x^2-20x+25\text{,}\) \(ac=75\) and the numbers are \(-15\) and \(-5\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 3x^2-20x+25 \amp= 3x^2-15x-5x+25 = (x-5)(3x-5) \end{align*}
    For \(2x^2-7x-15\text{,}\) \(ac=-30\) and the numbers are \(-10\) and \(3\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 2x^2-7x-15 \amp= 2x^2-10x+3x-15 = (x-5)(2x+3) \end{align*}
    For \(9x^2-3x-20\text{,}\) \(ac=-180\) and the numbers are \(-15\) and \(12\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 9x^2-3x-20 \amp= 9x^2-15x+12x-20 = (3x-5)(3x+4) \end{align*}
    For \(12x^2+28x+15\text{,}\) \(ac=180\) and the numbers are \(18\) and \(10\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 12x^2+28x+15 \amp= 12x^2+18x+10x+15 = (2x+3)(6x+5) \end{align*}
    Multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction and cancel the common factors \(x-5\text{,}\) \(3x-5\text{,}\) and \(2x+3\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \amp \dfrac{(x-5)(3x-5)}{(x-5)(2x+3)}\cdot\dfrac{(2x+3)(6x+5)}{(3x-5)(3x+4)}\\ \amp= \dfrac{6x+5}{3x+4} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    10.

    Divide the rational expressions. \(\dfrac{21w^2+w-2}{2w^2-17w-9}\div\dfrac{12w^2-5w-3}{8w^2-2w-3}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor each quadratic.
    🔗
    For \(21w^2+w-2\text{,}\) \(ac=-42\) and the numbers are \(7\) and \(-6\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 21w^2+w-2 \amp= 21w^2+7w-6w-2 = (3w+1)(7w-2) \end{align*}
    For \(2w^2-17w-9\text{,}\) \(ac=-18\) and the numbers are \(-18\) and \(1\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 2w^2-17w-9 \amp= 2w^2-18w+w-9 = (w-9)(2w+1) \end{align*}
    For \(12w^2-5w-3\text{,}\) \(ac=-36\) and the numbers are \(-9\) and \(4\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 12w^2-5w-3 \amp= 12w^2-9w+4w-3 = (4w-3)(3w+1) \end{align*}
    For \(8w^2-2w-3\text{,}\) \(ac=-24\) and the numbers are \(-6\) and \(4\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 8w^2-2w-3 \amp= 8w^2-6w+4w-3 = (4w-3)(2w+1) \end{align*}
    Multiply by the reciprocal and cancel the common factors \(3w+1\text{,}\) \(2w+1\text{,}\) and \(4w-3\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \amp \dfrac{(3w+1)(7w-2)}{(w-9)(2w+1)}\cdot\dfrac{(4w-3)(2w+1)}{(4w-3)(3w+1)}\\ \amp= \dfrac{7w-2}{w-9} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    11.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{2x}{3y^2}+\dfrac{3y}{4x}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The least common denominator is \(12xy^2\text{.}\) Rewrite each fraction with this denominator:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{2x}{3y^2}+\dfrac{3y}{4x} \amp= \dfrac{2x\cdot 4x}{3y^2\cdot 4x}+\dfrac{3y\cdot 3y^2}{4x\cdot 3y^2}\\ \amp= \dfrac{8x^2}{12xy^2}+\dfrac{9y^3}{12xy^2} \end{align*}
    Add the numerators over the common denominator:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{8x^2+9y^3}{12xy^2} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    12.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{2}{x^2-2x-3}-\dfrac{5}{x^2+5x+4}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor each denominator:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} x^2-2x-3 \amp= (x-3)(x+1)\\ x^2+5x+4 \amp= (x+1)(x+4) \end{align*}
    The least common denominator is \((x-3)(x+1)(x+4)\text{.}\) Rewrite each fraction with this denominator:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \amp \dfrac{2(x+4)}{(x-3)(x+1)(x+4)}-\dfrac{5(x-3)}{(x-3)(x+1)(x+4)} \end{align*}
    Combine the numerators, being careful to distribute the negative sign:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \amp= \dfrac{2x+8-5x+15}{(x-3)(x+1)(x+4)}\\ \amp= \dfrac{-3x+23}{(x-3)(x+1)(x+4)} \end{align*}
    The numerator does not share a factor with the denominator, so this is fully simplified.
