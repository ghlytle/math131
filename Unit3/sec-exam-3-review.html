    Worksheet 3.1 Exam 3 Review

    Review of rational expressions, rational equations, and polynomial long division. Assume all variables take values for which the expressions are defined.
    🔗

    1.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{-14x^2y^3}{63xy^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The GCF of \(14\) and \(63\) is \(7\text{.}\) Cancel the common powers of \(x\) and \(y\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{-14x^2y^3}{63xy^2} \amp= -\dfrac{2}{9}\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{x}\cdot\dfrac{y^3}{y^2}\\ \amp= -\dfrac{2xy}{9} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    2.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{60x^3z}{-64xyz^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The GCF of \(60\) and \(64\) is \(4\text{,}\) and the negative sign moves to the front:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{60x^3z}{-64xyz^2} \amp= -\dfrac{15}{16}\cdot\dfrac{x^3}{x}\cdot\dfrac{z}{z^2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{y}\\ \amp= -\dfrac{15x^2}{16yz} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    3.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{-30x^2y^2z^2}{-35xz^3}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The two negative signs cancel, and the GCF of \(30\) and \(35\) is \(5\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{-30x^2y^2z^2}{-35xz^3} \amp= \dfrac{6}{7}\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{x}\cdot y^2\cdot\dfrac{z^2}{z^3}\\ \amp= \dfrac{6xy^2}{7z} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    4.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{48ab}{84b^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The GCF of \(48\) and \(84\) is \(12\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{48ab}{84b^2} \amp= \dfrac{4}{7}\cdot a\cdot\dfrac{b}{b^2}\\ \amp= \dfrac{4a}{7b} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    5.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{54c^2d}{-78cd^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The GCF of \(54\) and \(78\) is \(6\text{,}\) and the negative sign moves to the front:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{54c^2d}{-78cd^2} \amp= -\dfrac{9}{13}\cdot\dfrac{c^2}{c}\cdot\dfrac{d}{d^2}\\ \amp= -\dfrac{9c}{13d} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    6.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{-40x^3y}{-24xy^4}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The negative signs cancel, and the GCF of \(40\) and \(24\) is \(8\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{-40x^3y}{-24xy^4} \amp= \dfrac{5}{3}\cdot\dfrac{x^3}{x}\cdot\dfrac{y}{y^4}\\ \amp= \dfrac{5x^2}{3y^3} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    7.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{x^2-4}{x^2+2x}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the numerator as a difference of squares and the denominator by taking out the GCF:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{x^2-4}{x^2+2x} \amp= \dfrac{(x-2)(x+2)}{x(x+2)} \end{align*}
    Cancel the common factor \(x+2\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{x-2}{x} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    8.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{x^2-36}{x^2-6x}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the numerator as a difference of squares and the denominator by taking out the GCF:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{x^2-36}{x^2-6x} \amp= \dfrac{(x-6)(x+6)}{x(x-6)} \end{align*}
    Cancel the common factor \(x-6\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{x+6}{x} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    9.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{2x^2+x-21}{(5x-1)(2x+7)}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the numerator with the \(ac\) method: \(ac=-42\text{,}\) and \(-6\) and \(7\) multiply to \(-42\) and add to \(1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 2x^2+x-21 \amp= 2x^2-6x+7x-21\\ \amp= 2x(x-3)+7(x-3)\\ \amp= (x-3)(2x+7) \end{align*}
    Cancel the common factor \(2x+7\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{(x-3)(2x+7)}{(5x-1)(2x+7)} = \dfrac{x-3}{5x-1} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    10.

    Simplify the rational expression. \(\dfrac{8x^2-10x-3}{(2x-3)(3x+5)}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the numerator with the \(ac\) method: \(ac=-24\text{,}\) and \(-12\) and \(2\) multiply to \(-24\) and add to \(-10\text{.}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 8x^2-10x-3 \amp= 8x^2-12x+2x-3\\ \amp= 4x(2x-3)+1(2x-3)\\ \amp= (2x-3)(4x+1) \end{align*}
    Cancel the common factor \(2x-3\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{(2x-3)(4x+1)}{(2x-3)(3x+5)} = \dfrac{4x+1}{3x+5} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    11.

    Perform the indicated operation. \(\dfrac{5x^2}{x+6}\cdot\dfrac{x^2-36}{x^2-6x}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the second fraction:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} x^2-36 \amp= (x-6)(x+6)\\ x^2-6x \amp= x(x-6) \end{align*}
    Rewrite the product and cancel the common factors \(x+6\text{,}\) \(x-6\text{,}\) and one power of \(x\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{5x^2}{x+6}\cdot\dfrac{(x-6)(x+6)}{x(x-6)} \amp= 5x \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    12.

    Perform the indicated operation. \(\dfrac{3x+6}{5y}\cdot\dfrac{x^2+4}{x^2+10x+16}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor where possible. The numerator \(x^2+4\) does not factor over the integers.
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 3x+6 \amp= 3(x+2)\\ x^2+10x+16 \amp= (x+2)(x+8) \end{align*}
    Rewrite the product and cancel the common factor \(x+2\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{3(x+2)}{5y}\cdot\dfrac{x^2+4}{(x+2)(x+8)} \amp= \dfrac{3(x^2+4)}{5y(x+8)} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    13.

    Perform the indicated operation. \(\dfrac{5x^4}{12x^2y^3}\div\dfrac{9}{5xy}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Change division to multiplication by the reciprocal:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{5x^4}{12x^2y^3}\cdot\dfrac{5xy}{9} \end{equation*}
    Multiply across and combine powers of each variable:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \amp= \dfrac{25x^5y}{108x^2y^3}\\ \amp= \dfrac{25x^3}{108y^2} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    14.

    Perform the indicated operation. \(\dfrac{7x^2y}{9xy^3}\div\dfrac{3x^4}{2x^2y^2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Change division to multiplication by the reciprocal:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{7x^2y}{9xy^3}\cdot\dfrac{2x^2y^2}{3x^4} \end{equation*}
    Multiply across and combine powers of each variable:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \amp= \dfrac{14x^4y^3}{27x^5y^3}\\ \amp= \dfrac{14}{27x} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    15.

    Perform the indicated operation. \(\dfrac{2x}{x-1}+\dfrac{3}{x}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The least common denominator is \(x(x-1)\text{.}\) Rewrite each fraction with it:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{2x}{x-1}+\dfrac{3}{x} \amp= \dfrac{2x\cdot x}{x(x-1)}+\dfrac{3(x-1)}{x(x-1)}\\ \amp= \dfrac{2x^2+3x-3}{x(x-1)} \end{align*}
    The numerator does not factor into anything that cancels, so this is fully simplified.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    16.

    Perform the indicated operation. \(\dfrac{3x}{x-4}-\dfrac{2}{x}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The least common denominator is \(x(x-4)\text{.}\) Rewrite each fraction with it:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \dfrac{3x}{x-4}-\dfrac{2}{x} \amp= \dfrac{3x\cdot x}{x(x-4)}-\dfrac{2(x-4)}{x(x-4)} \end{align*}
    Combine the numerators, distributing the negative sign:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \amp= \dfrac{3x^2-2x+8}{x(x-4)} \end{align*}
    The numerator does not share a factor with the denominator, so this is fully simplified.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    17.

    Identify the invalid solutions, identify the LCD, and solve: \(\dfrac{-3}{4x+5}=\dfrac{2}{5x-7}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Invalid solutions: \(4x+5=0\) gives \(x=-\frac{5}{4}\text{,}\) and \(5x-7=0\) gives \(x=\frac{7}{5}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    LCD: \((4x+5)(5x-7)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solve: Multiply both sides by the LCD (equivalently, cross-multiply):
    🔗
    \begin{align*} -3(5x-7) \amp= 2(4x+5)\\ -15x+21 \amp= 8x+10\\ 11 \amp= 23x\\ x \amp= \dfrac{11}{23} \end{align*}
    This value is not on the invalid list, so the solution is \(x=\dfrac{11}{23}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    18.

    Identify the invalid solutions, identify the LCD, and solve: \(\dfrac{7}{x+4}=\dfrac{3}{x-8}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Invalid solutions: \(x=-4\) and \(x=8\text{.}\)
    🔗
    LCD: \((x+4)(x-8)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solve: Multiply both sides by the LCD:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 7(x-8) \amp= 3(x+4)\\ 7x-56 \amp= 3x+12\\ 4x \amp= 68\\ x \amp= 17 \end{align*}
    This value is not on the invalid list, so the solution is \(x=17\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    19.

    Identify the invalid solutions, identify the LCD, and solve: \(n-\dfrac{2}{n}=\dfrac{17}{4}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Invalid solutions: \(n=0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    LCD: \(4n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solve: Multiply both sides by \(4n\text{:}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 4n\cdot n-4n\cdot\dfrac{2}{n} \amp= 4n\cdot\dfrac{17}{4}\\ 4n^2-8 \amp= 17n\\ 4n^2-17n-8 \amp= 0 \end{align*}
    This quadratic does not factor over the integers (the discriminant is \(289+128=417\text{,}\) which is not a perfect square), so use the quadratic formula:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} n \amp= \dfrac{17\pm\sqrt{(-17)^2-4(4)(-8)}}{2(4)}\\ \amp= \dfrac{17\pm\sqrt{417}}{8} \end{align*}
    Neither value is \(0\text{,}\) so both are valid. The solutions are \(n=\dfrac{17+\sqrt{417}}{8}\) and \(n=\dfrac{17-\sqrt{417}}{8}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    20.

    Identify the invalid solutions, identify the LCD, and solve: \(\dfrac{x}{x+1}-2=\dfrac{3}{x-3}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Invalid solutions: \(x=-1\) and \(x=3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    LCD: \((x+1)(x-3)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solve: Multiply every term by the LCD:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} x(x-3)-2(x+1)(x-3) \amp= 3(x+1)\\ x^2-3x-2(x^2-2x-3) \amp= 3x+3\\ x^2-3x-2x^2+4x+6 \amp= 3x+3\\ -x^2+x+6 \amp= 3x+3\\ 0 \amp= x^2+2x-3\\ 0 \amp= (x+3)(x-1) \end{align*}
    So \(x=-3\) or \(x=1\text{.}\) Neither is on the invalid list, so both are solutions.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    21.

    Identify the invalid solutions, identify the LCD, and solve: \(\dfrac{x}{x-4}-\dfrac{2}{x+3}=\dfrac{20}{x^2-x-12}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the denominator: \(x^2-x-12=(x-4)(x+3)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Invalid solutions: \(x=4\) and \(x=-3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    LCD: \((x-4)(x+3)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solve: Multiply every term by the LCD:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} x(x+3)-2(x-4) \amp= 20\\ x^2+3x-2x+8 \amp= 20\\ x^2+x-12 \amp= 0\\ (x+4)(x-3) \amp= 0 \end{align*}
    So \(x=-4\) or \(x=3\text{.}\) Neither is on the invalid list, so both are solutions.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    22.

    Identify the invalid solutions, identify the LCD, and solve: \(\dfrac{2x}{x-2}+\dfrac{15}{x^2-7x+10}=\dfrac{3}{x-5}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Factor the denominator: \(x^2-7x+10=(x-2)(x-5)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Invalid solutions: \(x=2\) and \(x=5\text{.}\)
    🔗
    LCD: \((x-2)(x-5)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solve: Multiply every term by the LCD:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 2x(x-5)+15 \amp= 3(x-2)\\ 2x^2-10x+15 \amp= 3x-6\\ 2x^2-13x+21 \amp= 0 \end{align*}
    Use the \(ac\) method: \(ac=42\text{,}\) and \(-6\) and \(-7\) multiply to \(42\) and add to \(-13\text{.}\)
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 2x^2-6x-7x+21 \amp= 0\\ 2x(x-3)-7(x-3) \amp= 0\\ (x-3)(2x-7) \amp= 0 \end{align*}
    So \(x=3\) or \(x=\dfrac{7}{2}\text{.}\) Neither is on the invalid list, so both are solutions.
    🔗
    🔗

    23.

    Is polynomial long division possible or not possible? Do not divide. \(\dfrac{3x^3-4x^2+8x+8}{x^2-2x+4}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Long division requires the degree of the numerator to be at least the degree of the denominator. The numerator has degree \(3\) and the denominator has degree \(2\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Since \(3\ge 2\text{,}\) the division is POSSIBLE.
    🔗

    31.

    Perform the polynomial long division. \(\dfrac{x^2+11x-64}{x-4}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Divide the leading terms: \(x^2\div x=x\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} x(x-4) \amp= x^2-4x\\ (x^2+11x-64)-(x^2-4x) \amp= 15x-64 \end{align*}
    Next, \(15x\div x=15\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 15(x-4) \amp= 15x-60\\ (15x-64)-(15x-60) \amp= -4 \end{align*}
    The quotient is \(x+15\) and the remainder is \(-4\text{,}\) so
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{x^2+11x-64}{x-4}=x+15-\dfrac{4}{x-4} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    32.

    Perform the polynomial long division. \(\dfrac{x^2-7x-76}{x+6}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Divide the leading terms: \(x^2\div x=x\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} x(x+6) \amp= x^2+6x\\ (x^2-7x-76)-(x^2+6x) \amp= -13x-76 \end{align*}
    Next, \(-13x\div x=-13\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} -13(x+6) \amp= -13x-78\\ (-13x-76)-(-13x-78) \amp= 2 \end{align*}
    The quotient is \(x-13\) and the remainder is \(2\text{,}\) so
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{x^2-7x-76}{x+6}=x-13+\dfrac{2}{x+6} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    33.

    Perform the polynomial long division. \(\dfrac{4x^3-x^2-2x+6}{x-2}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Divide the leading terms: \(4x^3\div x=4x^2\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 4x^2(x-2) \amp= 4x^3-8x^2\\ (4x^3-x^2-2x+6)-(4x^3-8x^2) \amp= 7x^2-2x+6 \end{align*}
    Next, \(7x^2\div x=7x\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 7x(x-2) \amp= 7x^2-14x\\ (7x^2-2x+6)-(7x^2-14x) \amp= 12x+6 \end{align*}
    Next, \(12x\div x=12\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 12(x-2) \amp= 12x-24\\ (12x+6)-(12x-24) \amp= 30 \end{align*}
    The quotient is \(4x^2+7x+12\) and the remainder is \(30\text{,}\) so
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{4x^3-x^2-2x+6}{x-2}=4x^2+7x+12+\dfrac{30}{x-2} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    34.

    Perform the polynomial long division. \(\dfrac{6x^3-2x^2+4x-3}{x+1}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Divide the leading terms: \(6x^3\div x=6x^2\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 6x^2(x+1) \amp= 6x^3+6x^2\\ (6x^3-2x^2+4x-3)-(6x^3+6x^2) \amp= -8x^2+4x-3 \end{align*}
    Next, \(-8x^2\div x=-8x\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} -8x(x+1) \amp= -8x^2-8x\\ (-8x^2+4x-3)-(-8x^2-8x) \amp= 12x-3 \end{align*}
    Next, \(12x\div x=12\text{.}\) Multiply and subtract:
    🔗
    \begin{align*} 12(x+1) \amp= 12x+12\\ (12x-3)-(12x+12) \amp= -15 \end{align*}
    The quotient is \(6x^2-8x+12\) and the remainder is \(-15\text{,}\) so
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \dfrac{6x^3-2x^2+4x-3}{x+1}=6x^2-8x+12-\dfrac{15}{x+1} \end{equation*}
