    1.

    Find the degrees of the following polynomials:
    🔗

    (a)

    \(4x^5-3x^2+6x-3\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} deg(4x^5) = 5 \\ deg(-3x^2) = 2 \\ deg(6x) = 1 \\ deg(-3) = 0 \end{gather*}
    The degree of the polynomial is \(5\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    \(\dfrac{1}{2}x^3-16x^2+\dfrac{4}{3}x-7\)
    🔗
    `
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} deg\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x^3\right) = 3\\ deg(-16x^2) = 2\\ deg\left(\dfrac{4}{3}x\right) = 1 \\ deg(-7) = 0 \end{gather*}
    The degree of the polynomial is \(3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    \(5x^2y+4x^3y - 3xy^2\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} deg(5x^2y) = 3 \\ deg(4x^3y) = 4 \\ deg(-3xy^2) = 3 \end{gather*}
    The degree of the polynomial is \(4\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (d)

    \(10x^3z^6 - 5x^4z^2 + 13x^8\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} deg(10x^3z^6) = 9 \\ deg(-5x^4z^2) = 6 \\ deg(13x^8) = 8 \end{gather*}
    The degree of the polynomial is \(9\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (e)

    \(8a^2b-\dfrac{1}{4}a^3b^2 + 12ab\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} deg(8a^2b) = 3 \\ deg\left(-\dfrac{1}{4}a^3b^2\right) = 5 \\ deg(12ab) = 2 \end{gather*}
    The degree of the polynomial is \(5\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (f)

    \(6y^3 + 3y^2-9y + 12\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} deg(6y^3) = 3\\ deg(3y^2) = 2\\ deg(-9y) = 1\\ deg(12) = 0 \end{gather*}
    The degree of the polynomial is \(3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    2.

    Perform the indicated operations:
    🔗

    (a)

    Add the polynomials: \((5x^3+6x^2-4)+(3x^2-7x+12)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Collecting like terms, we have
    \begin{gather*} 5x^3 + (6x^2+3x^2) + (-7x) + (-4+12)\\ 5x^3+9x^2-7x+8 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    Add the polynomials: \((-4x^4+x^3-10x^2+3x-9)+(x^4+x^3+2x^2+3x+6)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Collecting like terms, we have
    \begin{gather*} (-4x^4+x^4) + (x^3+x^3) + (-10x^2+2x^2) + (3x+3x) + (-9+6)\\ -3x^4 + 2x^3 -8x^2+6x-3 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    Subtract the polynomials: \((16x^2+5x-3)-(13x^2-2x-1)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    First, we distribute the negative, then collect like terms.
    \begin{gather*} 16x^2+5x-3 - 13x^2 + 2x+1\\ 3x^2+7x-2 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (d)

    Subtract the polynomials: \((x^3+3x-4)-(-2x^2 + 7x -6)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    First, we distribute the negative, then collect like terms.
    \begin{gather*} x^3+3x-4 + 2x^2 - 7x + 13\\ x^3 + 2x^2 - 4x + 9 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    3.

    Multiply the polynomials
    🔗

    (a)

    \((x-6)(x+7)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} x(x+7) - 6(x+7) \\ x^2 + 7x - 6x - 42\\ x^2 + x - 42 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    \((x-2)(x^2+6x+13)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} x(x^2+6x+13) - 2(x^2+6x+13)\\ x^3 + 6x^2+13x - 2x^2 - 12x - 26 \\ x^3 + 4x^2+x-26 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    \((x+1)(3x^2 - 8x + 9)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} x(3x^2 -8x+9) + 1(3x^2-8x+9)\\ 3x^3 - 8x^2+9x + 3x^2-8x+9\\ 3x^3 - 5x^2 + x + 9 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (d)

    \((4x+5)(7x-9)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} 4x(7x-9) + 5(7x - 9)\\ 28x^2 - 36x+ 35x - 45\\ 28x^2 - x - 45 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (e)

    \((2x+1)(4x^2-7x-1)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} 2x(4x^2-7x-1) + 1(4x^2 - 7x-1\\ 8x^3- 14x^2-2x + 4x^2 - 7x - 1 \\ 8x^3 - 10x^2 - 9x -1 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (f)

    \((3x-2)(x^2-10x+5)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{gather*} 3x(x^2-10x+5) -2(x^2-10x +5)\\ 3x^3 - 30x^2 + 15x - 2x^2+20x-10\\ 3x^3-32x^2+35x - 10 \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    4.

    Factor the following polynomials.
    🔗

    (a)

    \(x^3-x^2+4x-4\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Group the first two and last two terms together.
    \begin{equation*} (x^3-x^2) + (4x-4) \end{equation*}
    Next, extract the GCF of each group.
    \begin{equation*} x^2(x-1) + 4(x-1) \end{equation*}
    Extract the overall GCF.
    \begin{equation*} (x-1)(x^2+4) \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    \(x(7y-1) + 3(7y-1)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Extract the overall GCF
    \begin{equation*} (7y-1)(x+3) \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    \(2x^3-4x^2+7x-14\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Group the first two and last two terms together.
    \begin{equation*} (2x^3-4x^2) + (7x-14) \end{equation*}
    Next, extract the GCF of each group.
    \begin{equation*} 2x^2(x-2) + 7(x-2) \end{equation*}
    Extract the overall GCF.
    \begin{equation*} (x-2)(2x^2+7) \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (d)

    \(w(z+3)-5(z+3)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Extract the overall GCF
    \begin{equation*} (z+3)(w-5) \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (e)

    \(25a^2-36b^2\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    This is a difference of squares: \((5a)^2 - (6b)^2\)
    \begin{equation*} (5a+6b)(5a-6b) \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (f)

    \(15x^2+x-2\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Look for two numbers that multiply to make \((15)(-2)= -30\) and add to make \(1\text{.}\) The numbers \(6\) and \(-1\) work.
    🔗
    Next, we rewrite the polynomial.
    \begin{equation*} 15x^2 -5x+6x-2 \end{equation*}
    Now proceed by factoring by grouping.
    \begin{gather*} (15x^2-5x) + (6x-2)\\ 5x(3x - 1) + 2(3x-1)\\ (3x-1)(5x+2) \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (g)

    \(8x^2+2x-1\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Look for two numbers that multiply to make \((8)(-1) = -8\) and add up to make \(2\text{.}\) The numbers \(4\) and \(-2\) work.
    🔗
    Next, we rewrite the polynomial.
    \begin{equation*} 8x^2 + 4x - 2x - 1 \end{equation*}
    Now proceed by factoring by grouping.
    \begin{gather*} (8x^2+4x) + (-2x-1) \\ 4x(2x+1) - 1(2x+1)\\ (2x+1)(4x-1) \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (h)

    \(9y^2-16z^2\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    This is a difference of squares: \((3y)^2 - (4z)^2\text{.}\)
    \begin{equation*} (3y+4z)(3y-4z) \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    5.

    Perform the indicated operations.
    🔗

    (a)

    \((-2a^2)(3ab^2)(a^2b^3)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    We multiply the coefficients and use the exponent rules to collect the powers.
    \begin{equation*} (-2)(3)a^{2+1+2}b^{2+3} = -6a^5b^5 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    \(\left(\dfrac{1}{2}ab\right)(8a^3b^2)(-2a^3)\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    We multiply the coefficients and use the exponent rules to collect the powers.
    \begin{equation*} \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)(8)(-2)a^{1+3+3}b^{1+2} = -8a^7b^3 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    \((4x^2y^3)^2\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    We will use exponent rules to break up the power and then evaluate.
    \begin{equation*} (4)^2(x^2)^2(y^3)^2 = 16x^4y^6 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (d)

    \(\dfrac{-27x^3y^4}{3xy^3}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    We will divide the coefficients and apply the exponent rules to simplify.
    \begin{equation*} \left(\dfrac{-27}{3}\right)x^{3-1}y^{4-3} = -9x^2y \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (e)

    \((3x^4y^5)^3\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    We will use exponent rules to break up the power and evaluate.
    \begin{equation*} (3)^3(x^4)^3(y^5)^3 = 27x^{12}y^{15} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (f)

    \(\dfrac{20a^4b^6}{5ab^3}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    We will divide the coefficients and use exponent rules to simplify.
    \begin{equation*} \left(\dfrac{20}{5}\right)a^{4-1}b^{6-3} = 4a^3b^3 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    6.

    This question involves Polynomial Equations.
    🔗

    (a)

    Verify that \(x=1\) and \(x = -\dfrac{1}{5}\) are solutions to the equation
    \begin{equation*} (5x-2)^2-9 = 0 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    Solution.
    First, plug in \(x=1\) and simplify
    \begin{align*} (5(1)-2)^2-9 \amp =0\\ (5-2)^2 - 9 \amp =0\\ 3^2 - 9 \amp = 0\\ 9 - 9 \amp = 0\\ 0 \amp = 0 \end{align*}
    🔗
    We next plug in \(x = -\dfrac{1}{5}\) and simplify
    \begin{align*} \left(5\left(-\dfrac{1}{5}\right)-2\right)^2-9 \amp =0\\ (-1-2)^2 - 9 \amp =0\\ (-3)^2 - 9 \amp = 0\\ 9 - 9 \amp = 0\\ 0 \amp = 0 \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    Solve the polynomial equation \((7x-3)(6x+5) = 0\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    This polynomial is already factored, so we can use the zero product property.
    \begin{align*} 7x-3 = 0 \amp \phantom{vasdf}6x + 5 = 0\\ 7x = 3 \amp \phantom{vasdf}6x = -5\\ x = \dfrac{3}{7} \amp \phantom{vasdf} x = \dfrac{-5}{6} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    Solve the polynomial equation: \((y-2)(y+7) = -18\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Before solving the equation, we need to rewrite it into expanded form
    \begin{align*} y(y+7) - 2(y+7) \amp = -18\\ y^2 + 7x - 2y - 14\amp -18\\ y^2 + 5y + 4 \amp =0 \end{align*}
    🔗
    We can now factor the polynomial. We are looking for two numbers that multiply to make \((1)(4)=4\) and add up to \(5\text{.}\) The numbers \(1\) and \(4\) work. We then rewrite the polynomial and factor by grouping.
    \begin{align*} y^2 + y + 4y + 4 \amp = 0 \\ y(y + 1) + 4(y + 1) \amp = 0 \\ (y + 1)(y + 4) \amp = 0 \end{align*}
    🔗
    Now use the zero product property
    \begin{align*} y+1 = 0 \amp \phantom{vasdf} y+4 = 0 \\ y = -1 \amp \phantom{vasdf} y = -4 \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (d)

    Solve the polynomial equation \(x(3x-5) = 2\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Before solving the equation, we need to rewrite it into expanded form
    \begin{align*} 3x^2 - 5x \amp = 2\\ 3x^2 - 5x - 2 \amp =0 \end{align*}
    🔗
    We can now factor the polynomial. We are looking for two numbers that multiply to make \((3)(-2)=-6\) and add up to \(-5\text{.}\) The numbers \(1\) and \(-6\) work. We then rewrite the polynomial and factor by grouping.
    \begin{align*} 3x^2+x-6x-2 \amp = 0 \\ x(3x+1)-2(3x+1) \amp = 0 \\ (3x+1)(x-2)\amp = 0 \end{align*}
    🔗
    Now use the zero product property
    \begin{align*} 3x+1 = 0 \amp \phantom{vasdf} x-2 = 0 \\ x = -\dfrac{1}{3} \amp \phantom{vasdf} x = 2 \end{align*}
    🔗
